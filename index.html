User Help & Guide

👋 Welcome to the Math Lab Guide!

This interactive educational application helps second-grade students master place value (Base Ten Blocks) and money counting skills (Piggy Bank Coins) through hands-on visual modeling and immediate feedback.

📦

Base Ten Blocks Manipulative

  • 100 / 10 / 1

    Place Value Chart (Hundreds, Tens, Ones)

    The workspace is split into three columns: Hundreds (100s), Tens (10s), and Ones (1s). Spawning or dragging blocks into any column automatically snaps them into the correct place value.

  • Supply Panel

    Adding Blocks

    Click or drag Hundred Flats (100), Ten Rods (10), or One Units (1) from the right-hand supply panel directly into the workspace.

  • Regrouping

    Exchanging & Regrouping Tools

    Use the place value regrouping action buttons:

    • 🔒 Combine 10 Ones to 1 Ten: Exchanges 10 unit blocks for 1 ten rod (activates when 10+ ones are present).
    • 🔓 Break 1 Ten to 10 Ones: Decomposes 1 ten rod into 10 individual unit blocks.
    • 🔒 Combine 10 Tens to 1 Hundred: Exchanges 10 ten rods for 1 hundred flat (activates when 10+ tens are present).
    • 🔓 Break 1 Hundred to 10 Tens: Decomposes 1 hundred flat into 10 ten rods.
🪙

Piggy Bank Coins Manipulative

  • 4 Coins

    Coin Denominations (1¢, 5¢, 10¢, 25¢)

    Practice counting real money values using Pennies (1¢), Nickels (5¢), Dimes (10¢), and Quarters (25¢).

  • Heads & Tails

    Heads & Tails Supply Pairs

    The right-hand supply panel offers both Heads and Tails options for every coin denomination. Click or drag your choice to drop it into the Piggy Bank.

  • Interactive Flip

    Flipping Coins in the Bank

    Tap or click any dropped coin inside the Piggy Bank window to flip its face back and forth between Heads ↔ Tails with a realistic coin sound effect!

  • Live Summary

    Coin Count Summary Bar

    The bottom summary bar tracks the exact count of each coin type currently inside the bank.

🎯

Modes, Targets & Feedback

  • Modes

    Practice Mode vs. Quiz Mode

    Toggle between learning modes in the top navigation bar:

    • Practice Mode: Freeform building! The live total counter display updates instantly as you place items.
    • Quiz Mode: Self-assessment! The live total value is hidden so students can count independently.
  • Targets

    Setting Target Numbers & Ranges

    Use the 3 🎲 Random range buttons (e.g. 1–20, 1–130, 1–999 for Base Ten; 1¢–20¢, 1¢–50¢, 1¢–99¢ for Coins) to generate instant practice goals. Teachers can click ⚙️ Teacher Settings (Default PIN: 1234) to customize upper bounds or lock student target entry.

  • Feedback

    ✨ Check Answer & Confetti

    Click ✨ Check Answer to evaluate your work. Correct solutions trigger celebratory confetti and sound effects; incorrect attempts show helpful hints indicating how much to add or remove.

⚙️

Teacher Settings & Classroom Controls

  • Security

    🔒 PIN Verification & Security

    Access Teacher Settings by clicking the ⚙️ gear icon in the top header. A security prompt will ask for a 4-digit PIN (Default PIN: 1234). Teachers can change the PIN at any time inside the settings panel.

  • Bounds

    📦 Custom Upper Limits for Base Ten & Piggy Bank

    • Base Ten Upper Limit: Choose standard presets (1–20, 1–130, 1–999) or enter a custom maximum target number (e.g. max 50).
    • Base Ten Max Block Count: Limit the total physical blocks allowed on the workspace mat (e.g. max 50 blocks or Unlimited).
    • Piggy Bank Upper Limit: Choose standard presets (1¢–20¢, 1¢–50¢, 1¢–99¢) or enter a custom maximum coin value (e.g. max 25¢).
    • Piggy Bank Max Coin Count: Limit the total physical coins allowed inside the piggy bank window (e.g. max 20 coins or Unlimited).
  • Presets

    🎲 Range Button Visibility Control

    Toggle individual range buttons (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3) on or off so students only see the difficulty levels suitable for their current lesson.

  • Locking

    🔒 Student Target Input Lock

    Enable Lock target text box to prevent students from manually typing arbitrary numbers into the target input. When locked, students must use the teacher-configured 🎲 Random generator buttons.

  • Storage

    💾 Automatic Storage & Persistence

    All teacher settings are automatically saved in browser storage (localStorage) and remain active on classroom devices across sessions. Click Reset Defaults to restore factory settings at any time.

🧹

Workspace Controls & Deletion

  • Trash Can

    Deleting Items

    Drag any block or coin to the 🗑️ Drag here to delete trash zone at the bottom-right of the workspace, or hover over an item and click the red ✕ delete button.

  • Reset Tools

    Clear Board & Reset All

    Use Clear Board to empty the current workspace without clearing your target, or click Reset All to restore all settings to default.

  • Audio

    Sound Effects (🔊/🔇)

    Toggle sound effects on or off using the speaker icon in the top-right header.

💡 Handy Tips for Whiteboards & Touchscreens:

  • Touchscreen & Touch Board Friendly: Tapping any candidate item in the right supply panel automatically spawns it directly into the workspace.
  • Infinite Supply: You can drag as many blocks or coins as needed from the supply panel!
  • Offline Ready: The app runs 100% offline in any modern web browser without internet connection needed.
